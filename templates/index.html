ABOUT THE MODEL

How WeatherPredict Works

🌦️

WeatherPredict uses a Decision Tree Classifier trained on the project's weather dataset. It learns patterns between 10 weather measurements and the target weather condition.

For a new set of measurements, the tree follows learned decision rules and predicts one of 6 classes: Sunny, Partly Cloudy, Cloudy, Rainy, Thunderstorm, or Foggy.
